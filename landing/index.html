Caring isn't the hard part. Remembering is.

Friends share what's coming up in their lives. Then work, travel and busy weeks happen, and the moment to ask "how did it go?" quietly passes. Most tools for this feel like a spreadsheet for your friendships. KeepClose is just one line and a day.

Research suggests people tend to underestimate how much others appreciate being reached out to (Liu et al., Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2022).

How it works

1. Jot it down"Ask Mariana how the interview went" — Friday.
2. Forget about itAt most one gentle reminder a day, only when there's something to remember.
3. Reach outSend the message yourself, in your own words. Tap "Asked" if you like.

Ask Mariana: how the interview went

Also: birthdays and one-off dates like moving day, and an optional "check in now and then" nudge for people you'd like to keep close. No scores, no streaks, no guilt.

Private by design

  • Everything is stored on your phone. There's no account and no server.
  • KeepClose never reads your messages and never asks for access to your contacts.
  • Lock-screen reminders don't show names or notes unless you choose to.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Questions

Is it a "personal CRM"?

No. There are no profiles to maintain or dashboards to check. You add a person when there's something to remember about them.

Does it send messages for me?

Never. It can open your messaging app with the number filled in; you write and send.

Will it nag me?

At most one notification a day, only on days with something to remember. You can turn it off and still see everything in the app.

What if I lose my phone?

Your data is included in your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) if you have that turned on. KeepClose has no copy.

Does it use AI?

No. Reminders are simple and predictable, and nothing leaves your phone.

How much does it cost?

The beta is free. We haven't decided on pricing; the core will stay free.

Which phones?

iPhone and Android. The app is in early testing and not in the stores yet.
