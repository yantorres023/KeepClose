## Summary
KeepClose stores everything you enter **only on your device**. We (the publisher) do not
receive, collect, sell or share your data. There is no account and no server.

## What the app stores on your device
- People you add: name, optional note, optional phone number.
- Reminders you create ("remember to ask"), dates (birthdays, events), and moments you log.
- Your settings (reminder time, notification detail level).

## Contacts
If you choose "Pick from contacts", your phone's system picker hands KeepClose only the
single contact you select (name and phone number). KeepClose does not request access to
your address book and never reads other contacts.

## Messages and calls
KeepClose never reads your messages, call history or social media. "Message" and "Call"
open your phone's own apps; you write and send everything yourself.

## Notifications
Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. By default they don't show
names or notes on the lock screen. You can change this in Settings.

## Data we collect
None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not connect to
the internet.

## Backups
If your device's backup service (e.g. iCloud Backup, Google backup) is enabled, it may
include KeepClose's data. Those backups are governed by Apple's or Google's terms and your
device settings.

## Deleting your data
Delete a person from their page, delete everything from Settings → Delete all data, or
uninstall the app.

## Children
KeepClose is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

## Changes
We'll update this page and the "Last updated" date if anything changes. If we ever add a
feature that sends data off your device, it will be optional and explained before use.
