# BRAND

**No trademark clearance has been performed.** Name checks below are basic web searches
(2026-09-25) and are not legal advice.

## Name conflicts found
- "Keep Close" — UK App Store app for bereavement keepsakes/urns [S27]. Different category, but identical name in app stores.
- "EverClose" (family location tracking), "AppClose" (co-parenting) — similar-sounding, different categories.
- "Check Up: Keep Friends Close" — friendship reminder app using the phrase "Keep Friends Close" [S18]. Closest conflict in *our* category.

## Recommendation
Keep **KeepClose** as the working/code name for the beta (renaming code is wasteful), but
**do not treat it as the final store name** until a trademark search is done. For store
listings use a descriptive suffix to reduce confusion: **"KeepClose: Remember to Ask"**.

## Alternatives (for a later decision, all unchecked)
1. **Asked** — "Did you ask how it went?" Short, action-oriented.
2. **Remember to Ask** — descriptive, SEO-friendly, weak as a trademark.
3. **Followthrough** — the promise, but sounds like productivity.
4. **Kindly** — tone-perfect, likely crowded.
5. **Checkin (Check-in)** — generic.
6. **Heard** — "they'll know you heard them".

## Tagline
Primary: **"Remember to ask."**
Supporting: "Remember the small things that show people you care." · "Private. Gentle. On your phone."

## Voice
Warm, brief, human. Second person. Never clinical, never guilt. Celebrate small acts
("Nice."/"Lovely."). No exclamation-heavy hype, no productivity jargon, no "CRM".

## Visual direction
Calm and soft: deep sage green (#3F6B5B seed) with warm neutrals, Material 3, generous
spacing, rounded cards, one accent. No gamification visuals (no streak flames, meters,
badges).

## Icon brief
A simple speech bubble whose tail forms a small heart or a gentle check mark, in sage on
a warm off-white background. Must read at 29×29 pt, no text, no photos of people,
distinct from messaging-app icons (avoid solid green-bubble look that resembles
Messages/WhatsApp). Deliverables: 1024×1024 iOS icon, Android adaptive icon
(foreground + background layers), monochrome Android 13 themed icon.
Current state: default Flutter icon — **must be replaced before store submission.**
