# SOURCES

All accessed 2026-09-25 via web search. **Method limitation:** the build environment's
network policy blocked direct page fetches for most domains (Reddit, HN, App Store,
Google Play, NCBI, ScienceDaily, getdex.com, americansurveycenter.org). Evidence below
was gathered from search-engine result excerpts, not full-page reads. Every claim that
depends on a source is therefore rated at most MEDIUM confidence unless it is
corroborated by multiple independent sources. No reviews were read in bulk; review
"themes" are indicative, not counted.

IDs are referenced from the other research docs as `[S#]`.
