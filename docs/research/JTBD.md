# JOBS TO BE DONE

All jobs are HYPOTHESES (UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS).

## Thesis A — Relationship maintenance
- Job: "When months slip by, help me notice I haven't been in touch, so the friendship doesn't fade by accident."
- Trigger: none in daily life (the app must create it) → weak.
- Current alternatives: memory, social feeds, calendar repeats, a dozen cadence apps.
- Evidence: phenomenon strong [S3, S4]; bottleneck is often hesitation not memory [S1]; category crowded [A5].
- Verdict: **supporting feature**, not the wedge.

## Thesis B — Promises / follow-ups
- Job: "When someone tells me about something coming up in their life, help me remember to ask about it afterwards, so they feel I actually listened."
- Trigger: a specific conversation moment (strong, recurring, external).
- Data needed: person + one line + a day. Minimal.
- Current alternatives: phone Reminders app, memory, iOS 26 AI suggestions [S26].
- Why a dedicated tool could win: person context (see all open "asks" and past notes when you open a friend), gentle people-centric copy, one-tap "I asked / reached out", no message scanning.
- Evidence: indirect [A7, A8]; weakest direct evidence but best fit with product principles.
- Verdict: **primary wedge**.

## Thesis C — Important moments
- Job: "Don't let me miss the dates that matter to people I love."
- Trigger: calendar dates (built-in).
- Alternatives: OS birthdays, free birthday apps [S21].
- Verdict: **supporting feature** (birthdays + one-off dates), no premium on it.

## Combined job statement
"Help me remember the small things that show people I care — what they told me, what's
coming up for them — and nudge me gently at the right moment, without turning friendship
into admin."

## Adjacent wedge considered
- "Conversation starters / what to say" — addresses the awkwardness barrier [S1] directly but risks AI-drafted messages and inauthenticity. Deferred; the follow-up itself *is* the conversation starter.
