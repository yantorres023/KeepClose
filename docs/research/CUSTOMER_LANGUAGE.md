# CUSTOMER LANGUAGE

No interviews were conducted. The phrases below are paraphrases or short fragments that
appeared in search-result excerpts of public sources; they are NOT verified verbatim quotes
from identifiable users and must not be used as testimonials.

## Phrases observed (paraphrased, with source)
- "stay in touch with the people who matter most" — dominant category language [S14, S15, S18].
- "without the mental load" — Friend Reminder positioning [S19].
- Reminders described as "annoyingly" persistent — Garden review snippet [S14].
- Syncing "a manual chore" — Contacts Journal review snippet [S20].
- Import "glitchy", cancelled subscription — Dex review snippets [S12].
- Friend interactions should feel like "memories" not "tasks to tick off" — essay paraphrase [S25].
- Keep outreach small: "not more than two friends a week" — advice paraphrase [S25].
- Barriers to reaching out: "awkward", "they might not want to hear from me", guilt — study participants [S1].

## Words to use (INFERENCE)
- "remember to ask", "check in", "thinking of", "you wanted to", "reach out", "catch up", "people who matter", "on your phone only".

## Words to avoid
- CRM, contacts pipeline, lead, touchpoint, cadence (in UI), overdue, streak, score, health, fading, dormant, at risk, "you haven't talked to X in N days".

## Notification tone examples (chosen)
- "Ask Mariana how the interview went?"
- "Thinking of Lucas? Could be a nice day to say hi."
- "Ana's birthday is today."
- Default lock-screen text (private mode): "You have 2 gentle reminders today."
